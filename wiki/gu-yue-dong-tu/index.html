General Information
Name in the translationGu Yue Dong Tu (Ch. 9)
RoleUncle of Fang Yuan and Fang Zheng (Ch. 9)
ClanGu Yue (Ch. 9)
Parents’ inheritanceHeld by the uncle and aunt (Ch. 9)

Gu Yue Dong Tu is the uncle of the brothers (Ch. 9). In Chapter 9 he offers to adopt Fang Zheng and offers Fang Yuan two hundred primeval stones to set up his own household (Ch. 9).

He and the aunt plan to frame Fang Yuan and expel him before he is sixteen, when he could claim the family inheritance (Ch. 9). Their scheme uses Shen Cui (Ch. 9, Ch. 11).

The aunt is not named in Chapter 9 (Ch. 9).
