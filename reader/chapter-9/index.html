About the story

After five hundred years in a world ruled by cultivation and Gu, Fang Yuan faces the end of his life surrounded by enemies. With the Spring Autumn Cicada, he returns to his youth on Qing Mao Mountain—carrying the memories of a lifetime and a resolve that has not changed.

In a world where Gu Masters nurture, refine and use extraordinary living creatures, he begins again. Clan expectations, scarce resources and hidden inheritances stand between him and the path he intends to forge.
